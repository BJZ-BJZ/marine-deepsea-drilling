Robert Podgorney, John McLennan, and Joe Moore (2018). *Utah FORGE: Drilling Data for Student Competition*. Idaho National Laboratory, Geothermal Data Repository. DOI: [10.15121/1495411](https://doi.org/10.15121/1495411). [Source record](https://gdr.openei.org/submissions/1113).

The unchanged processed Pason CSV is included with SHA-256 `065468bf3c41098541fa7d0f3b8754b32e659b0877ed0a12efca6072f022189b`. Official file URL: [processed Pason log](https://gdr.openei.org/files/1113/Well_58-32_processed_pason_log.csv).

The research-derived files apply filtering, causal lags, train/test assignments, trained-model predictions, SHAP summaries and statistical envelope calculations to this geothermal dataset. These are adaptations; they do not imply endorsement by the dataset authors. The original final training program is not included, so this compact edition verifies archived outputs rather than claiming end-to-end retraining.
